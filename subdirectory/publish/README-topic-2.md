## Suggested structure

### 1. Hook

- Analogy: onion layers / camera (cornea+lens vs film vs processing).
- Promise: tunics first, then “one number rules refractive thinking” (24 mm).

### 2. Three tunics (basic)

1. **Outer fibrous:** cornea (~anterior 1/6) + sclera (~posterior 5/6) — protection and shape; cornea ~43 D refractive power.
2. **Middle vascular (uvea):** iris, ciliary body, choroid — nutrition, pupil, aqueous, choroidal blood supply.
3. **Inner neural:** retina — phototransduction.

### 3. Deeper: outer and middle (intermediate)

- Cornea vs sclera; where they meet → **limbus** (tease next section).
- Iris / ciliary body / choroid roles in one tight paragraph each.

### 4. Axial length & refractive error (advanced, very shareable)

- Normal axial length ~**24 mm**.
- Rule of thumb from your content: ~**−3 D per 1 mm increase** (myopia), ~**+3 D per 1 mm decrease** (hyperopia).
- **Biometry pearl:** ~**0.3 D refractive surprise per 0.1 mm** axial length error around cataract/IOL planning.

### 5. The limbus: critical zone

- ~1 mm wide; cornea–sclera–conjunctiva junction.
- **Palisades of Vogt** — limbal stem cells; trabecular meshwork; Canal of Schlemm; surgical landmark.
- **LSCD narrative:** chemical injury, Stevens-Johnson — conjunctivalization, vascularization, opacity (emotional + educational).

### 6. Diagram-guided tour (optional section)

- Cross-section walk: cornea, anterior chamber, iris, lens, vitreous, macula/fovea, optic disc — match your SVG labels.

### 7. Close

- Tie back: structure → refractive surgery, glaucoma, retina — “anatomy is the index to the clinic.”

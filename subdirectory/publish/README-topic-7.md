## Suggested structure

### 1. Hook

- Contrast cornea (fixed power) vs lens (**variable power** via shape).

### 2. Basic anatomy

- Transparent, **avascular** (nutrition from aqueous).
- **Biconvex**; anterior vs posterior capsule; nucleus vs cortex (as in your diagram).

### 3. Zonules (suspensory ligament)

- From ciliary body to lens equator — mechanical link for accommodation.

### 4. Accommodation mechanism

- Ciliary muscle contraction → zonular slack → lens rounds → more power for near (simplify with diagram from lesson).

### 5. Lifelong lens growth

- New fibers at periphery; older fibers compressed centrally → harder nucleus over decades.

### 6. Presbyopia

- Reduced accommodative amplitude; typically **40s** narrative (general education).

### 7. Cataract (conceptual)

- Lens opacity with aging/surgery/disease — tie to growth and protein changes without overstating.

### 8. Close

- “Cataract surgery isn’t ‘fixing a surface’ — it’s replacing an aging optical element.”
